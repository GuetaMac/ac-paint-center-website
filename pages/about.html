About AC Paint Center

Color and craftsmanship, right here in Alaminos.

AC Paint Center is a paint supplies store and painting contracting business along Maharlika Highway in Brgy. Alaminos, Laguna — right in front of Puregold Alaminos. Led by JR Cascabel, we give homeowners, businesses, and fellow contractors a reliable place to buy quality paint, and a team they can trust to get the job done right.

Our Story

We may still be a young business, but we bring focus and honesty to every project — whether it's a can of paint sold over the counter or a full residential or commercial painting job. From our physical store to the sites we paint, our aim is the same: solid workmanship, fair prices, and service our customers can count on.

Every job, big or small, gets the same attention to detail — because in this business, the finish is what people remember.
